KumonScan · SMS Privacy Policy

KumonScan SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective 17 August 2026. Program: KumonScan parent SMS. Sender: (424) 227-5975.

These terms cover the KumonScan text program for parents and guardians of students at a Kumon learning center. By agreeing to receive texts, you consent to informational messages from KumonScan at (424) 227-5975 about your child’s attendance and related center communication.

How you opt in (verbal script used at the front desk)

Consent is collected in person at enrollment. Staff read the following and enter the mobile number in KumonScan Admin only after a clear yes. SMS is optional. Enrollment can continue without it.

“We use KumonScan to text you when your child checks in and when they are ready for pickup, plus occasional staff notes and a weekly progress digest from KumonScan at (424) 227-5975. Typical volume is 2 texts per visit and at most 1 weekly digest. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Privacy Policy: kumonscan.vercel.app/sms-privacy.html. Terms: kumonscan.vercel.app/sms-terms.html. Do you want to receive these texts at this number?”

What you receive

  • Check-in notice when staff sign the student in at the desk.
  • Pickup notice when the student checks out.
  • Occasional one-to-one staff messages (reminders, schedule notes). You may reply; staff see replies in KumonScan.
  • A weekly visit and worksheet digest when there is attendance or progress to report.
  • A one-time parent-portal sign-in link if you request access (expires in 15 minutes, single use).

This program does not send marketing, advertising, or promotional offers.

Message frequency

Typical volume is 2 messages per visit plus at most 1 weekly digest, with occasional staff texts. Frequency follows attendance.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

HELP and STOP

Reply HELP to (424) 227-5975 for help, or contact the Kumon center front desk.

Reply STOP to (424) 227-5975 to cancel all KumonScan SMS. After STOP you will not receive further texts unless you opt in again.

Carrier liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your wireless carrier and device.

Privacy

Mobile numbers are used only to send this program’s messages. See the KumonScan SMS Privacy Policy (https://kumonscan.vercel.app/sms-privacy.html). Mobile information will not be shared with third parties for marketing.

Contact

Kumon center front desk, or text HELP to (424) 227-5975.
