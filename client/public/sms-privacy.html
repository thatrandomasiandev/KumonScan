KumonScan SMS Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026. Program: KumonScan parent SMS. Sender: (424) 227-5975.

KumonScan is the attendance system used at a Kumon learning center. This policy covers mobile numbers and related data used to send parent text messages. It does not replace any privacy notice the Kumon center already provides for enrollment records.

What we collect

For SMS we store:

  • The parent or guardian mobile number entered during student enrollment.
  • Student first and last name, used in the message body (for example check-in and pickup notices).
  • Attendance times from KumonScan sessions, used to compose those notices.
  • Optional staff-composed message text and inbound replies in the Admin Messages thread.

How we use it

We use this information only to deliver KumonScan SMS: check-in notices, pickup notices, occasional staff reminders, a weekly visit and worksheet digest, and a one-time parent-portal sign-in link when requested. Twilio, Inc. is the messaging service provider that transmits the texts. Twilio is not given numbers for its own marketing.

No sharing for marketing

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell phone numbers. We do not use this campaign for advertising, coupons, or unrelated offers.

Message frequency

Typical volume is 2 messages per center visit (check-in and checkout) plus at most 1 weekly digest. Staff may send occasional additional texts. Frequency follows the student’s attendance, not a marketing cadence.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on the recipient’s wireless plan.

Opt out

Reply STOP to (424) 227-5975 to cancel. Reply HELP for help, or contact the Kumon center front desk. After STOP we will not text that number unless the parent opts in again.

Contact

Questions about this SMS program: Kumon center front desk, or text HELP to (424) 227-5975. Terms of service: https://kumonscan.vercel.app/sms-terms.html.
