Two ways in. One record.

Some students carry their QR code, some do not, and the record has to hold up either way. Both paths write the same timeapi.io-stamped session.

From the desk

Staff search the roster by name, pick Math, Reading, or Both, and tap Check in. The subject choice sets the allowance: 30 minutes for one subject, 60 for both. Check-out is one tap on the same row.

  1. Search the student's name
  2. Pick Math, Reading, or Both
  3. The timer starts on the floor list

From the kiosk

Students scan their own QR code on a shared tablet. The first scan of the day checks them in; scanning again checks them out. Duplicate reads inside 3 seconds are ignored, so an excited eight-year-old can't check in twice.

Built around Kumon's 30/60-minute session rule.

A Kumon session runs 30 minutes for one subject and 60 for both. KumonScan times every open session against that allowance. When a student passes it, the row turns red and shows the exact overage, like +8 min, so staff see who to release without doing clock math.

Timestamps come from timeapi.io, never the device clock. If timeapi.io is unreachable, check-in and check-out are rejected rather than recorded with a guess. A parent asking about last month's sessions gets times the center can stand behind.

What's inside today.

Everything below is shipped and running in the product, not a roadmap.

Absence lists
Each student has scheduled weekdays. Desk generates the list of students expected that day who never checked in, so follow-up calls happen the same afternoon.
Attendance reports
Monthly or rolling 12-month exports as CSV or PDF: visits, total minutes, and overtime count per student. The dashboard charts average check-ins per weekday over the past 28 days against the scheduled roster.
CRM roster import
Upload the Kumon CRM student export (TSV or CSV). Matching names update, new names are created. The export has no schedule column, so bulk apply sets weekdays (MWF, TTh, Mon–Fri, or custom) across the roster in one step.
Parent notifications and replies
Check-in and check-out texts go to parents through an Android phone at the center running the KumonScan gateway app. Replies land in Admin as a two-way thread per student. No per-message SMS vendor fees.
Staff time clock and payroll
Staff clock in and out with the same timeapi.io timestamps as student sessions, one open shift per person enforced by the database. The payroll report totals hours and gross pay from hourly rates over any date range, exported as CSV.
Capacity by weekday
Set seats per weekday in Admin. Desk shows today's expected students against capacity and flags the day when it's oversubscribed.
Remote sessions
Remote check-ins are recorded alongside in-person ones with the same timers, overtime flags, and reports, marked with a mode chip on the desk roster.
Admin access control
Staff pages sit behind a password with httpOnly session cookies and rate-limited login. The kiosk stays open for students; the records do not.
